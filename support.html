Roomsmith Support

Scan a room, place real furniture in it, and see a photograph of how it could look.

Roomsmith uses your iPhone's depth sensor to measure a room, keeps the scan on the phone, and makes pictures of that room redesigned the way you describe. Everything you scan and everything you make stays on the device.

Scanning needs an iPhone Pro

Rooms are measured with a LiDAR scanner, and Apple fits one only to the Pro models. On an iPhone without it, Roomsmith opens and rooms you have already scanned still work, but no new room can be scanned.

When a scan comes out poor

The scanner needs to see a surface properly before it believes in it. Most bad scans come from moving too quickly.

  • Walk the whole room slowly, and give each wall a moment rather than sweeping past it.
  • Stay about a metre or two back from what you are scanning. Very close and very far both read badly.
  • Turn the lights on. The depth sensor copes with dim rooms better than the camera does, but the photographs taken during the scan still need light.
  • Open doors and pull chairs out from under tables, so the shapes are separable.
  • Windows, mirrors and glass tables confuse any depth sensor. Expect them to come out approximate.

If the result is still wrong, scanning the room again is usually faster than working around it. A scan you no longer want can be deleted from the room list.

Pictures are made over the network

The redesigned photographs are not made on the phone. Roomsmith sends what it needs to a small service, which passes it to the image model and sends the picture back, so making a picture needs a working internet connection. A fast picture usually takes about fifteen seconds, and the slower, better one about two minutes.

Without a connection you can still open your rooms, browse your furniture library and look at every picture you have already made. They are stored on the phone, not fetched.

If a picture fails, it is nearly always the connection or a busy model. Trying again a minute later is the right first move.

What happens to your rooms

Scans, photographs, floor plans and generated pictures live on your iPhone. Deleting a room deletes them, and deleting the app deletes all of it. The privacy policy sets out exactly what leaves the device when a picture is made.
